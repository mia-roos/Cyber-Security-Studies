### 24 November 2026 – From theory to practice

During the past two weeks, I have learned about authentication, MFA, 
passkeys, hashing, encryption and password management.

Initially, I found public-key cryptography quite challenging. 
Understanding the different roles of public and private keys 
required time and practical examples. Encrypting and decrypting 
a message with Kleopatra helped me connect theory with practice.

One of my most important realizations was how frequently I use 
cryptography in everyday life without being aware of it.

I also improved my personal cybersecurity by setting up a 
synchronized password database across my computer and iPhone. 
This experience highlighted the importance of balancing security, 
usability and availability.

I am increasingly interested in how these concepts can be applied 
in healthcare, where confidentiality, integrity, trust and 
continuity of care are particularly important.

I want to continue developing my technical cybersecurity skills 
and deepen my understanding of secure authentication and 
digital trust.
