### What I Learned

The peer-review process helped me understand how important clear documentation, reliable scientific sources, and critical thinking are in cybersecurity research.

I also explored the **META risk management model**:

- **Mitigate:** Reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.
- **Eliminate:** Remove the source of a risk.
- **Transfer:** Transfer or share part of the risk.
- **Accept:** Make an informed decision to accept and monitor a risk.

I learned that organisations can combine several strategies when managing cybersecurity risks.

I also practised interpreting risk calculations based on the probability of an incident and its estimated financial consequences.

### Reflection: Cybersecurity and Healthcare

One of my most important observations today was that **cybersecurity risk management involves much more than technical solutions**.

In healthcare, cyber incidents can affect patient safety, continuity of care, and access to essential information. Therefore, financial calculations alone are not sufficient when evaluating cybersecurity risks.

My background in healthcare and nursing science helps me approach these questions from the perspective of patient safety and organisational responsibility.
